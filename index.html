SecondSpin

Garages stay full because nobody knows what anything is worth. SecondSpin prices your stuff with category depreciation curves - phones fall 40% a year, instruments barely 12% - factors in condition and age, and gives the only answer that matters: sell it now, keep it, or stop listing it and let it go.

  • Resale estimate from real decay curves, not vibes
  • 6-month trajectory - see what waiting costs you
  • Verdicts: sell soon (losing fast), keep (holding), donate (past the floor)
  • A running history of everything you've priced
